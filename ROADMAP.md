## Next up (suggested order)

1. **Career follow-ups** (see `FOLLOWUPS.md` §3): difficulty tiers unlocked by career victories to offset talent power, heirloom upgrades, an "unlocks" layer (spells/items gated by level).
2. **Balance pass**: the class parity pass is done (see Done and Known issues; parity is measured on the top 20% of smart-shop builds, see `Goals.md`). Left: the Fighter is still a little ahead on its best builds (73% vs 55–58% on fresh seeds) and Shield Wall is its strongest pick (+3.1 waves lift); the Necromancer depends on Grave Curse (+6.6 lift, every top build has it) and its all-build average is still lowest (10.1 waves vs ~14); weakest spells per class: Second Wind and Whirlwind (Fighter), Arcane Missiles and Frost Nova (Mage), Bone Armor, Bone Nova and Bone Spear (Necromancer), Blood Pact and Shadow Bolt (Warlock). War Cry was left alone (+1.1 lift, not an outlier). Rerun `tools/sweep.py` after each change. Also: no new enemy type appears after wave 4, so waves 5–20 only scale numbers; add late enemies or an elite/boss wave.
3. **Sound:** a few placeholder effects (hit, pickup, level-up) through the Master bus the volume setting already controls.
4. **Real art:** swap the placeholder SVGs for final art through the same `Sprite` fields (Kenney CC0 packs or commissioned). Animation means a `SpriteFrames` (walk/attack/hit) next to `Sprite` and an `AnimatedSprite2D` child, or drawing the current frame from `_Draw()` like now. Pick the final style (pixel vs vector, view angle) before buying or commissioning, since every asset depends on it. Projectiles, pickups and effects are still `_Draw()` shapes.

## Backlog (ideas, not yet scheduled)

- Element tags (fire, frost) that items and spells can scale with. Fire burns (damage over time), frost slows. Weapon × element = build variety.
- Rarity follow-ups: a Legendary tier (orange) with a unique effect rather than bigger numbers; offering a rarer copy of gear you already wear (hidden today); heirloom upgrades as a tier bump per victory (`FOLLOWUPS.md` §3, §6).
- Fill spell slot 4 during the run: e.g. a spell offer in the shop after wave 2 or 3 (the "mix" answer to when spells get chosen). Each class now has 8 spells; more would need the spell select grid rethought (scrolling or a 5th column).
- Spell upgrades in the shop (Fireball +radius, Chain Lightning +1 jump), tying spells into the item economy.
- Selling items outright (replacing gear already refunds half); item drops during waves.
- Shared (classless) weapons in the catalog; weapon-specific bonuses per class (Brotato style).
- Vacuum pickup (Vampire Survivors): a rare drop that pulls all gold on screen to the player.
- Hand-authored waves (`WaveData` Resources) replacing the `WaveSettings` formula; an elite or boss on the final wave.
- More stats: minion count/HP/duration (cooldown reduction, HP regen and dodge are done). Maybe a Luck or Harvesting-style economy stat.
- Minion upgrades (more skeletons, longer lifetime); a Necromancer item that raises from every kill. A taunt (minion that pulls aggro from further away).
- Settings follow-ups: key rebinding, a colorblind-friendly rarity palette, UI scale.
- Sound and music (the Master volume setting already drives the audio bus).

## Known issues and tuning notes

- **Class balance sweep (20 waves, 56 builds × 3 seeds per class, `--move=smart --shop=smart --autocast`).** Metric: per class, the top 20% of builds (11 of 56) by average waves cleared; win rate / average waves, then all builds. Before the pass: Fighter 100% / 20.0, all 17.9 / 74%; Warlock 91% / 19.4, all 14.1 / 48%; Necromancer 42% / 13.9, all 8.2 / 10%; Mage 36% / 15.9, all 11.0 / 11%. After: Fighter 82% / 19.2, all 14.1 / 42%; Warlock 82% / 18.9, all 13.6 / 40%; Mage 61% / 18.0, all 14.0 / 25%; Necromancer 58% / 16.4, all 10.1 / 17%. Picking the top 11 from the same 3 seeds flatters them (lucky seeds); replaying the after top 11 on fresh seeds 101–103 gives Fighter 73% / 16.9, Mage 58% / 16.7, Warlock 58% / 15.8, Necromancer 55% / 15.2, the fairer numbers. With 3 seeds a build's win rate moves in steps of 33%, so differences under ~10 points are noise. Spell lift after (waves with − without): Fighter Shield Wall +3.1, Second Wind −2.7, Whirlwind −2.2; Mage Blizzard +3.9, Arcane Missiles −1.4, Frost Nova −1.4; Necromancer Grave Curse +6.6, Bone Armor −2.0; Warlock Siphoning Aura +1.6, Blood Pact −1.5. Items (within class, smart shop, so biased by its weights): Sacrificial Sickle +4.7 for the Warlock, Book of the Dead +3.0 for the Necromancer, Chainmail and Troll Heart lead the shared items. Defensive spells still read low partly because the bot casts them on cooldown. The first sweep (5 seeds, before Mage/Necromancer HP and the spell buffs): Fighter 17.1 / 70%, Warlock 11.1 / 24%, Mage 5.8 / 0.4%, Necromancer 5.4 / 1%. A full sweep with 3 seeds takes about an hour on the Mac (Fighter builds survive longest).
- Autoplay's default `--move=circle` dies by wave 2–3 with every class (it doesn't dodge); that's now the "bad player" baseline. `tools/simulate.sh -n 10 -m circle,smart` (seeds 1–10, cheapest shop) win rate / average wave, circle → smart: Fighter 0% / 2.5 → 90% / 5.0, Mage 0% / 2.8 → 50% / 4.8, Warlock 0% / 3.2 → 100% / 5.0, Necromancer 0% / 2.9 → 80% / 4.8. Read these as relative numbers (the bot isn't a human), but the Warlock is clearly the easiest and the Mage the hardest to survive with.

- Restart (R, Choose class, Abandon run) goes back to class select in the same mode; the Back button there returns to mode select. Retry same class in career skips the talents screen, so points earned by the last run wait until the player goes through Choose class.
- Career balance is unplayed: talents only add power and waves don't scale with career level (see `FOLLOWUPS.md` §3). Autoplay Fighter, `--god`, seed 2, same seed each run: 184 → 215 kills once talents and an heirloom kicked in.
- Difficulty in waves 4–5 hasn't been tuned since the new enemy types were added.
- Casters die in two hits from a wave 6–8 Brute (10 contact damage × 1.75–2.05) even at 24 HP, so many caster runs end early with few hits taken; top caster builds that survive wave 10 mostly win. Flat HP closes the gap only so far: more caster armor, a Brute wind-up or percent-based potions are options (`FOLLOWUPS.md` §10).
- The Fighter now has the same HP as the Mage and Necromancer (24) and less than its old 30; what keeps it alive now is knockback from its melee weapon and Shield Wall. If that feels wrong, the alternative is HP back up with weapons cut further (`FOLLOWUPS.md` §10).
- Healing numbers are first guesses: heart +2 (heals when hurt, else into the reserve) at 3% (Brutes 15%), potion +10 once per wave. A full run drops roughly 5–8 hearts, about one full reserve. +10 is most of the Mage's 16 HP, so the potion may be strong for the Mage and weak for the Fighter (30 HP); a percent-of-max heal is an option.
- Consumable prices (Health Potion 10, Fury 8, Swiftness 6) and effects are first guesses. Autoplay's default `cheapest` strategy never buys potions (use `--buy-potion`). Moving hearts into the reserve barely changed Autoplay survival (seeds 1–5, average death wave: Fighter 2.2 → 2.4, Mage 2.8 → 2.8, Warlock 3.0 → 2.6, Necromancer 2.4 → 2.4).
- New spell numbers are first guesses, set by comparing with the existing four, not by playtest. War Cry (+40% Damage and Attack Speed for 6s, 15s cooldown) and Meteor (40 damage) are the likeliest to be too strong. Charge's drawn lane is narrower than its hit width (40 each side).
- Auto-cast Charge picks a direction toward the nearest enemy and dashes the full range, which can carry you into a crowd. It's manual by default for that reason.
- Crit item values are first guesses: crit chance is worth less per point than Damage% (+10% crit at ×1.5 ≈ +5% damage), so the items give bigger numbers.
- Feel knobs not tuned by playtest yet: `ScreenShake` per ability (Feel group), `Player.HurtShake`, `ShakeCamera.MaxOffset`, Wand knockback 40. Damage numbers stay frozen on screen behind the defeat message.
- Gear and new weapon numbers are first guesses. With seed 2, no shop and `--god`, kills over a full run were: Sword 182, Spear 229, Greatsword 223, Wand 235, Frost Scepter 241, Staff 253. The upgrades beat the starters, but Spear and Greatsword are close, though the Greatsword also costs the off hand.
- Autoplay's `cheapest` shop strategy happily buys a weapon or armor that's worse than what it wears. That's fine for a test bot, but don't read balance into which gear it ends up with.
- Warlock/Necromancer numbers are first guesses. God-mode, no-shop runs (seed 2), total damage dealt over 5 waves: Fighter ~3.5k, Mage 4.1k–6.5k by spell pick, Warlock 4.2k–5.4k, Necromancer 4.0k–5.9k (minions 0.7k–3.3k of it; Bone Golem alone ~2.1k). Without `--god` over seeds 1–5, Autoplay's average death wave: Fighter 2.2, Necromancer 2.4, Mage 2.8, Warlock 3.0 (3.6 with Crimson Nova + Blood Pact + Drain Life, ~100 HP stolen per run).
